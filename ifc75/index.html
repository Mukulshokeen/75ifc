About The Institute of Indian Foundrymen

The Institute of Indian Foundrymen (IIF) is the apex body representing the foundry industry in India. Established in 1950, IIF has been instrumental in the growth and development of the foundry sector.

The 75th Indian Foundry Congress marks a milestone platinum jubilee celebration, bringing together industry leaders, innovators, and experts from across the globe.
